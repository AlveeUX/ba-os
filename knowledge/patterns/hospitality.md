# Hospitality Checklist

Questions for hotel, resort and serviced-apartment projects, on top of `common-gaps.md`. `/ba-start` adds this file to the profile when the project is in hospitality; `/ba-check` raises a `Q` item for each question that applies and is not answered yet.

Learned from two hotel projects in 2026: a multi-hotel booking website (P-2026-001) and a hotel-group KPI and reporting RFP practice project. No client names, prices or personal data.

## Channels and availability
- Which booking sites (for example Booking.com, Agoda, Expedia) sell the rooms, and what share of bookings does each bring?
- Is availability shared with them automatically (channel manager or direct connection) or typed in by hand? Who does it, and how often?
- What happens when the same room is sold twice? Who decides which guest keeps it?
- Is there a property management system (PMS) at each hotel? Is it the same system everywhere, and does the new product replace it, read from it or ignore it?

## Payments and deposits
- Is a deposit taken? How much, and does it depend on how far away the arrival date is?
- Which local payment methods are needed (for example mada, SADAD, Apple Pay), and how long does each take to confirm?
- Is a room held while the guest pays? For how long, and what happens if payment confirms after the hold ends?
- Cancellation, no-show and date-change rules: what exactly happens at the boundary (for example exactly 7 days before, or on arrival day)?
- Are card numbers ever taken by phone or written down? (card-security risk)

## Seasons and pricing
- Which seasons change prices or availability (for example Hajj, Ramadan, Eid, school holidays), and who confirms the dates each year?
- Does any hotel open or sell only in some seasons?
- Who changes prices, and does a change need approval?

## Several hotels
- What can head office see and change, compared with each hotel?
- Which differences between hotels are legitimate local practice and must be kept?
- Should the product launch at one or two hotels first (pilot) before the rest? (L-002)

## KPIs and reporting
- How is each KPI calculated at each hotel today — occupancy, ADR (average daily rate), RevPAR (revenue per available room), direct-booking share? Do the hotels agree? (L-001)
- Is a KPI counted by number of bookings, room nights or money?
- Who owns each definition, and who approves a change to it?

## Region, language and regulation (GCC)
- Arabic and English: which comes first, and who writes and checks each language? Right-to-left layout is needed for Arabic.
- VAT rate and whether shown prices include it; e-invoicing (for example ZATCA in Saudi Arabia) — who on the client side confirms the rules?
- Personal-data rules and where guest data must be stored (data residency).
- Must guest or occupancy data be reported to a tourism or government authority?
