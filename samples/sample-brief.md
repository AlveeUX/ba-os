Subject: New website for our clinic

Hi,

We run Smile Point Dental, a family dental clinic with two branches in the city. Our current website was made in 2019 and looks old. Most patients still book by phone, and our receptionists spend a lot of their day on calls. Last month we missed many calls during busy hours and we think we lost patients because of it.

We want a modern, fast website where patients can book appointments online. We also need an app so patients can see their appointments and get reminders.

Things we need:

- Online booking for both branches
- Patients should choose their dentist
- Reminders before the appointment
- A page for each service with prices
- Staff should be able to manage the bookings
- It should be easy to use for older patients

We would like to launch before Eid. Our budget is limited, so please keep it simple.

Thanks,
Dr. Farhana Rahman
Owner, Smile Point Dental
