Client Answers

1. [Q-001] Business goal
"We want to increase direct bookings on our own platform instead of always going through booking sites like Booking.com. We're paying them too much commission. Success means 30% of bookings come direct within the first year. Also, guests expect a modern experience — they should be able to book quickly and manage their reservations themselves, not call us."

2. [Q-014] Decision maker and budget
"I make the final decisions. I have about 120,000 Saudi Riyals (~$32,000 USD) for the platform, but I'm flexible if it's worth it. The deadline is hard: 4 months maximum. My team will help with content and testing."

3. [Q-007] Timeline and scope
"The 4 months starts now (late September). But we don't need everything on day one. A first version with the basics — a nice website, room search, booking, and payment — is fine. We can add the admin dashboard and reports later. What matters is guests can book online by end of December."

4. [Q-002] Current website problems
"It's slow, looks outdated, not mobile-friendly. Guests complain. What should be kept: the hotel photos are good, the location information is useful, and the contact details. Everything else needs to be new. Modern means fast, mobile-first, simple to use."

5. [Q-012] Migration from old site
"Our current website is hosted with a small Saudi hosting company. Content (descriptions, photos, some guest reviews) should move over. We don't have old booking data — guests called or booked through third-party sites. We can keep the same domain name (something like saudhotels.com or similar). My IT person can handle that."

6. [Q-003] One platform or six?
"One platform for all 6 hotels. But each hotel should have its own page. The hotels are similar — all 3-star, but different room types and slightly different pricing. Al Riyadh and Al Jeddah are our flagship hotels. We manage everything centrally from the head office here."

7. [Q-013] Booking system today
"Most bookings come through Booking.com or Agoda. We also have a WhatsApp number where guests message us directly. Inside the hotels, front desk uses a spreadsheet to track rooms — very manual. No system. The new platform should be our single source of truth."

8. [Q-005] Payment and currencies
"Guests should pay a deposit up front — usually 25-50% of the total. We need to accept credit cards (Visa, Mastercard), and some Saudi guests use local payment methods. Mostly Saudi Riyal, but some international guests pay in USD. We don't have a payment provider yet — you'll recommend one."

9. [Q-006] Cancellation rules
"Free cancellation up to 7 days before arrival. After that, we keep the deposit. No partial refunds — it's all or nothing. If a guest needs to change dates instead of cancel, that's free anytime."

10. [Q-008] Customer accounts
"Guests should see their booking, check-in dates, room details, and be able to cancel or modify dates. They can book without creating an account, but if they do, they can manage their reservations easily. We want to collect their email and phone so we can send confirmations and reminders."

11. [Q-004] Admin roles
"Three roles: (1) Head office — can see all 6 hotels, manage all bookings, change prices and availability, view reports. (2) Hotel manager — only sees their own hotel's bookings and can manage them. (3) Front desk staff — can only view bookings, not change anything. We have 2 people at head office, and about 1-2 staff at each hotel."

12. [Q-009] Booking management
"We need to view all bookings, see guest details, confirm or cancel them, track which ones have paid the deposit, and send reminders before arrival. Hotel managers should handle cancellations for their own hotel. If a guest calls to cancel, someone needs to mark it in the system quickly."

13. [Q-010] Reports
"Monthly reports showing: total bookings, revenue (by hotel and all together), occupancy rate, cancellations, and no-shows. Head office reads these. We need to see per-hotel data and combined data. Nothing fancy — Excel is fine, or PDF."

14. [Q-011] Languages
"Everything in both Arabic and English: the customer website, confirmation emails, payment pages, the admin dashboard, reports, everything. My head office team writes and approves the Arabic — I'll get them involved. The English should be clear and professional.
