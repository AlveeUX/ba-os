A. Goal and numbers

Q-035: Which first-year target is right — 30% or 50% direct? Counted by bookings or money? When does year one start?

50% is what we want to reach by end of year one, not by December. Right now it's 10% direct. By December, maybe 20%. By end of next year, 50%. Count it by money, not by number of bookings — a high-value booking matters more than a walk-in. Year one starts when we launch, so if we launch in December, year one runs Dec 2026 to Dec 2027.

Q-057: 50 bookings a month sounds low for 380 rooms. Is it per hotel, or per week, or real total?

It's the real total across all six hotels, right now. We're not at full capacity. Jeddah and Riyadh do maybe 20 bookings a month each. Dammam, Medina, Abha are slower — 5 each. Makkah is seasonal (only Hajj time, so maybe 30 bookings in one month, zero the rest of the year). So yeah, 50 total is realistic for now. But with the new platform and direct bookings, we expect to grow to 200 a month by year end.

New Q-3: Do phone and walk-in bookings count as "direct"? Breakdown of channels in last 12 months?

Yes, phone and walk-in count as direct — they're not going through Booking.com or Agoda. Last 12 months: roughly 60% Booking.com, 30% Agoda, 10% direct (phone, email, walk-in). So out of ~600 bookings a year, 360 from Booking.com, 180 from Agoda, 60 direct.

B. Staff roles and logins

Q-037: Which roles exist? Head office vs. hotel manager vs. front desk — where do you, Fatima, and head office fit?

Three roles:

Me (Yusuf): Head office, owner. I should see everything — all hotels, all reports, ability to change prices and policies.
Fatima (my manager): Also head office. She runs day-to-day operations across all six hotels. Same access as me.
Hotel manager (one per hotel): Each hotel has a manager (like the manager in Jeddah). They see only their own hotel's bookings, can mark payments and no-shows, but can't change prices or policies — I approve that.
Front desk staff (multiple per hotel): View bookings, type in phone bookings, mark check-ins. No access to other hotels.
Guests: Can book, view their own bookings, cancel/change within rules.

So really, it's 5 roles: Owner (me), Head office manager (Fatima), Hotel manager (6 of them), Front desk staff (many), Guest.

Q-038: Does a hotel manager see only their own hotel, or all six? Can all staff see all hotels?

Hotel manager: only their own hotel. Front desk: only their own hotel. Fatima and me: all six hotels. Guests: only their own booking.

Q-039: May front desk change bookings (type in phone bookings, mark payments, record no-shows), or only view?

They may change some things:

Type in phone bookings: YES
Mark payment received: YES (for phone/card bookings)
Record check-in: YES
Record no-show: YES
Record phone cancellation: YES
Change booking dates: NO — only hotel manager can do that

One login for me and Fatima. Six separate logins is too much hassle. One login that shows all six hotels in a dashboard is much better.

In the system: Mark phone bookings as "deposit paid" (because the rest is due later). Walk-ins: mark as "paid in full" once they hand over the money.

E. How things work today

Q-14: How does a Booking.com or Agoda booking reach your front desk? How do you stop double-selling?

Today, I get an email from Booking.com and Agoda. I forward it to Fatima. She types it into a shared Excel spreadsheet and marks that room as "booked" for those dates. If two bookings come in for the same room on the same dates, we:

Check the email timestamps — first one wins
Contact the second guest and offer them a different room or date
Or if they really want those dates, we offer them a nearby hotel

It's messy and manual. That's why we need the system — to prevent double-sells in the first place.

Q-15: When a guest books by phone, WhatsApp, or email, how is it confirmed and paid?

Phone: I or Fatima takes the call, get their card details, charge the deposit on the spot. Send a confirmation SMS or WhatsApp.
WhatsApp: They message us, we reply with a payment link (via a payment service), they click and pay, we confirm.
Email: They email a booking request, we reply with a payment link, same as WhatsApp.

No receipt is issued yet — we just keep a note.

Q-16: Is there one spreadsheet per hotel or one shared? Who updates it?

One shared spreadsheet for all six hotels (one sheet per hotel inside it). Fatima and the hotel managers can edit it. It's stored on Google Drive. It's our single source of truth right now, and it's a mess because people sometimes forget to update it.
