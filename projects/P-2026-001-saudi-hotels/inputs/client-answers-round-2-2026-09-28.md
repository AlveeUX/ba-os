Section B: Money and payments

Q-006 [NEW]: Who receives the money — one central account or each hotel's own?

One central account for now. We'll split it between hotels in the accounting system, but everything goes to head office first. That way, we control cash flow.

Q-007: What decides whether the deposit is 25% or 50%?

25% for bookings 7+ days before arrival. 50% for bookings less than 7 days. Full payment for walk-ins and phone bookings — they pay when they arrive or reserve by phone.

Q-008: Which local Saudi payment methods do your guests use?

Most are Saudi nationals, so SADAD (bank transfer), credit card (Visa/Mastercard), and Apple Pay. Some international guests use card. WhatsApp payments from guests is common too, but that's manual. We don't use cash online.

Q-009: For US dollar payments — are prices shown in both currencies?

Some international guests ask about USD. Show both SAR and USD, but charge in SAR. We use today's exchange rate (1 USD = 3.75 SAR roughly). We don't want to manage two currencies, so USD is just for reference.

Q-010: What if a guest books less than 7 days before arrival, doesn't show up, or changes to a costlier period?

Less than 7 days: They pay 50% deposit. If they don't show up, we keep it.
No-show: We keep the deposit, they lose it.
Changes to costlier period: They pay the difference before arrival.
Free cancellation: Only if cancelled more than 7 days before.
Free date change: Only if changed more than 7 days before. After that, it costs.
Refunds: Go back to the same payment method within 48 hours.

Q-011 [NEW]: If a payment fails, or a guest leaves halfway, what happens?

If payment fails, the booking is not confirmed. Room is held for 2 hours, then released. If a guest leaves halfway, they've already paid the deposit or full amount, so no refund. If two guests want the last room, first one to complete payment gets it.

Q-012 [NEW]: What do you already have ready for payment account — business docs, bank account? Talking to a payment provider?

We have all business documents. We're set up with a Saudi business bank account. We haven't talked to a payment provider yet — you'll recommend one, right?

Q-013: Does 120,000 SAR include hosting, payment fees, content, translation, running and support for first year?

Yes, all of it. Hosting, payment gateway fees, translation of descriptions, running the platform, support for the first year. After year one, we pay separately.
